Company Work Policy

Office Timing

Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed.

Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves, 12 sick leaves, and 15 earned leaves annually.

Dress Code

Smart casuals are expected from Monday to Thursday. Casuals are permitted on Fridays.

Work Ethics

Maintain professionalism, respect diversity, and adhere to the company's code of conduct.

Laptop Policy

Company laptops are for official use only. Ensure data security and report damages immediately.

Internet Usage

Use internet responsibly. Accessing unauthorized or inappropriate sites is strictly prohibited.
